# Cohort Analysis: Customer Retention and Churn Patterns

## Definition
-Cohort Analysis is a data analytics technique that groups customers based on a shared characteristic or event, such as the month of their first purchase. 
These groups, known as cohorts, are then tracked over time to analyze customer behavior, retention rates, engagement levels, and churn patterns.
-Instead of analyzing all customers together, Cohort Analysis helps businesses study how different groups of customers behave over a specific period. 
This makes it easier to identify customer retention trends, measure churn rates, and evaluate the effectiveness of business strategies.

## Purpose of Cohort Analysis
* Measure customer retention over time
* Identify customer churn patterns
* Track customer engagement levels
* Compare the behavior of different customer groups
* Support data-driven business decisions

## Cohort Retention Matrix
A Cohort Retention Matrix is a table that shows how many customers from a specific cohort continue to remain active in subsequent months.
The rows represent customer cohorts based on their first purchase month, while the columns represent the number of months after acquisition. 
The matrix helps visualize customer retention rates and highlights when customer drop-off occurs.

## Churn Patterns
Customer churn refers to customers who stop purchasing or engaging with a business over a period of time.By analyzing churn patterns through Cohort Analysis, 
businesses can identify when customers are most likely to leave and understand the factors contributing to customer loss.

## Studying churn patterns helps businesses:
* Identify high-risk customer groups
* Detect stages where customer drop-off is highest
* Improve customer retention strategies
* Increase Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)
* Reduce customer acquisition costs

-In this project, churn patterns will be identified using the Cohort Retention Matrix by observing how customer retention rates decline across different months.

## Use in This Project
In this project, customer transaction data will be grouped into cohorts based on the month of their first purchase. Using these cohorts,
a Cohort Retention Matrix will be created to calculate customer retention rates across different months.
The results will help identify customer drop-off points, understand retention behavior, and support Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) analysis.

## Expected Outcome
The Cohort Analysis will provide insights into customer retention trends, churn behavior, and long-term customer value. 
These insights will help businesses improve retention strategies, reduce churn, and increase overall profitability.
