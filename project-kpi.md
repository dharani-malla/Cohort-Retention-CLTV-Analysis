# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## KPI Definition
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are measurable metrics used to evaluate the success and performance of a business. They help organizations track progress towards their goals and make data-driven decisions.

## KPIs Used in This Project:
### 1. Customer Retention Rate
Measures the percentage of customers who continue to make purchases over a specific period.
### 2. Customer Churn Rate
Measures the percentage of customers who stop purchasing or engaging with the business.
### 3. Average Order Value (AOV)
Represents the average amount spent by customers per order.
### 4. Purchase Frequency
Measures how often customers make purchases within a given period.
### 5. Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)
Estimates the total revenue a business can expect from a customer throughout their relationship with the company.

## Importance of KPIs:
* Measure business performance
* Monitor customer retention and churn
* Support strategic decision-making
* Improve customer experience
* Increase profitability and growth

## Expected Outcome
These KPIs will help evaluate customer behavior, retention trends, and long-term customer value. The insights gained will support better
business decisions and improve overall business performance.
