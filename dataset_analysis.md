# Dataset Overview and Key Attributes

## Dataset Name
E-Commerce Data (Online Retail Dataset)

## Source
Kaggle – E-Commerce Data by Carrie1

## Dataset Link:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/carrie1/ecommerce-data

## Dataset Description
This dataset contains transactional records from a UK-based online retail company between December 2010 and December 2011. The company primarily sells 
unique all-occasion gifts, and many of its customers are wholesalers.
The dataset includes customer transactions, product information, purchase quantities, invoice details, pricing information, and customer locations.
It is widely used for customer behavior analysis, retention analysis, churn analysis, and Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) studies.

## Why This Dataset Was Selected
* Contains real-world e-commerce transactions
* Suitable for Cohort Retention Analysis
* Supports Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) analysis
* Helps identify customer retention and churn patterns
* Useful for customer behavior analysis

## Key Attributes
* InvoiceNo – Unique transaction identifier
* StockCode – Unique product identifier
* Description – Product description
* Quantity – Number of products purchased
* InvoiceDate – Date and time of transaction
* UnitPrice – Price per product unit
* CustomerID – Unique customer identifier
* Country – Customer location country

## Expected Use in the Project
The dataset will be used to perform customer retention analysis through Cohort Analysis. Customers will be grouped based on their first purchase date,
and a Cohort Retention Matrix will be created to analyze retention trends and customer churn behavior.
The dataset will also support Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) calculations by analyzing customer purchase frequency and transaction value.

## Expected Outcome
The analysis will provide insights into customer retention patterns, customer churn behavior, and long-term customer value. These insights can help businesses improve customer retention strategies and increase profitability.
