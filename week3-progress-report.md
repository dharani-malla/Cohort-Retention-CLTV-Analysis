# Week 3 Progress Report

## Project Title
Cohort Retention Analysis and Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) Analysis Using E-Commerce Data

## Introduction
The objective of Week 3 was to perform Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) analysis on segmented customer groups to evaluate long-term customer value across different regions.
The analysis focused on comparing customer segments using Average Order Value (AOV), Purchase Frequency, Historical CLTV, and customer performance metrics to 
support strategic business decision-making.

## Week 3 Progress Summary
During Week 3, the team focused on customer segmentation, segment-wise customer value analysis, Historical CLTV calculation, customer performance comparison,
and business insight generation. The primary goal was to identify high-value customer segments and evaluate customer performance across different regions.

## Work Completed

### 1. Customer Segmentation
* Customers were grouped into region-wise segments.
* Segmented datasets were prepared for customer value analysis.
* Data validation was performed to ensure segmentation accuracy.
* Segmented datasets were exported for further analysis.

### 2. Segment-wise Customer Value Analysis
* Average Order Value (AOV) was calculated for each customer segment.
* Purchase Frequency was calculated across all customer segments.
* Purchasing behavior was compared across different regions.
* Summary tables and visualizations were generated for customer value metrics.

### 3. Historical Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) Analysis
* Historical CLTV was calculated for each customer segment.
* Customer segments were compared based on Historical CLTV.
* High-value, medium-value, and low-value customer segments were identified.
* Region-wise CLTV comparison charts were generated.

### 4. Customer Segment Performance Analysis
* Customer segment performance reports were prepared by integrating Total Customers, Total Orders, Total Revenue, AOV, Purchase Frequency, and Historical CLTV.
* Region-wise performance comparisons were performed.
* Revenue and customer value metrics were analyzed across different customer segments.
* Comparative visualizations and summary tables were generated.

## Key Insights and Learnings
* Customer segmentation provides a clearer understanding of customer purchasing behavior.
* Historical CLTV helps identify the most valuable customer segments.
* Combining Revenue, AOV, Purchase Frequency, and CLTV provides a comprehensive evaluation of customer performance.
* Customer value analysis supports data-driven business strategies and retention planning.

## Challenges Encountered
* Integrating multiple analytical outputs into a unified performance report.
* Comparing customer segments using multiple business metrics.
* Ensuring consistency across segmented datasets and calculated metrics.
* Interpreting customer performance for meaningful business insights.

## Conclusion
Week 3 objectives were successfully completed. Customer segmentation, Historical CLTV analysis, customer performance comparison, and business insight generation
