# Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) Overview

## What is Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)?
Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) is a metric that estimates the total revenue a business can expect from a customer throughout their entire relationship
with the company.
CLTV helps businesses understand the long-term value of customers and identify which customer segments contribute the most to business growth and profitability.

## Why is CLTV Important?
* Measures long-term customer value
* Helps identify high-value customers
* Supports customer retention strategies
* Improves marketing decision-making
* Increases business profitability

## Factors Affecting CLTV
* Purchase Frequency
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Customer Retention Rate
* Customer Lifespan
* Customer Churn Rate

## Benefits of CLTV
* Helps businesses focus on valuable customers
* Improves customer acquisition strategies
* Supports personalized marketing campaigns
* Assists in revenue forecasting
* Enhances customer retention efforts

## Use in This Project
In this project, CLTV will be used to estimate the long-term value of customers based on their purchase behavior and transaction history. 
The analysis will help identify valuable customer segments and support business decision-making.

## Expected Outcome
The CLTV analysis will provide insights into customer value, retention trends, and profitability. 
These insights can help businesses improve customer relationships, increase retention, and maximize revenue.
