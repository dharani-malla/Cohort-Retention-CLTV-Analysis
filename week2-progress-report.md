# Week 2 Progress Report

## Project Title
Cohort Retention Analysis and Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) Analysis Using E-Commerce Data

## Introduction
The objective of Week 2 was to perform Cohort Retention Analysis and Customer Value Analysis using the cleaned e-commerce dataset. 
The project focused on understanding customer retention behavior, evaluating customer value metrics, and generating business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## Week 2 Progress Summary

During Week 2, the team focused on cohort analysis, retention analysis, and customer value analysis. Customer cohorts were created using Cohort Month and Invoice Month features. 
A Cohort Retention Matrix was generated and retention rates were calculated to study customer retention behavior over time. Customer value metrics such as Average Order Value (AOV),
Purchase Frequency, and Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) were calculated to evaluate customer profitability and long-term business value. Additional analyses including Top Customers
by Revenue, Country Revenue Analysis, and Cohort Performance Analysis were performed to generate business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## Work Completed

### 1. Cohort Analysis and Data Preparation
* Cohort Month and Invoice Month features were created.
* Data preprocessing and validation checks were completed.
* Customer cohorts were formed based on first purchase month.
* Cohort-ready dataset was prepared and shared with the team.
* Dataset summary statistics were generated.

### 2. Retention Analysis
* Cohort Retention Matrix was generated.
* Customer retention rates were calculated across cohorts.
* Retention trends were analyzed using visualizations.
* Dashboard insights were prepared using retention metrics.
* Retention summary statistics were documented.

### 3. Customer Value Analysis
* Average Order Value (AOV) was calculated.
* Purchase Frequency was determined.
* Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) was estimated.
* Customer value metrics were analyzed to understand long-term revenue contribution.

### 4. Revenue and Performance Analysis
* Top Customers by Revenue were identified and visualized.
* Country-wise Revenue Analysis was performed.
* Cohort Performance Analysis was conducted using retention metrics.
* Best-performing and low-performing cohorts were identified.
* Business insights were generated from customer and revenue analyses.

## Key Insights and Learnings
* Customer retention generally decreases over time across most cohorts.
* High-retention cohorts contribute positively to Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV).
* A small group of customers contributes a significant portion of total revenue.
* AOV, Purchase Frequency, and CLTV are valuable indicators of customer profitability.
* Cohort Analysis helps businesses understand customer engagement and retention patterns.

* Generating meaningful business insights from analytical results.
* Coordinating analytical tasks across team members.
